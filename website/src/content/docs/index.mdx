## Pick your job \{#start-here\}

- [Install a package][first-package] now and switch its versions
- [Pin a toolchain][first-project]: declare a set of tools in `ocx.toml`, for a repository or as the global toolchain
- Give [CI][first-project-ci] the same tools, with [GitHub Actions][github-actions]
- [Put a tool on your PATH][global-toolchain] in every shell
- [Publish your own tool][publishing]
- Provision [Bazel][bazel] or a [dev container][devcontainer]
- Work [offline][offline] or through a [mirror][mirror]
