ocx fetches pre-built tools from any OCI registry and runs them in a clean, reproducible environment.

```sh
curl -fsSL https://setup.ocx.sh/sh | sh
```

That installs ocx. Other shells and a download-only route are on the [install page][install].

## Start here \{#start-here\}

Three short pages take you from an empty machine to a pinned project.

- [Install ocx][install] puts the binary on your `PATH` and checks it.
- [Install your first package][first-package] runs a tool, installs two versions and switches between them.
- [Pin your first project's tools][first-project] locks a repository's tools so everyone runs the same builds.

## Use ocx every day \{#everyday\}

- [Choose a version][choose-versions] of a tool, by tag or by digest.
- [Set up a project toolchain][project-toolchain] for a repository.
- [Configure ocx][configure] for a private registry, a mirror or an offline machine.

## Publish and integrate \{#publish\}

- [Publish a package][publishing] to your own registry.
- [Use ocx in CI][integration], in containers and from scripts.

## Look things up \{#look-up\}

The [command line][cmd-ref], [environment variables][env-ref] and [project file][project-file] pages list every flag, variable and key. The [concepts][storage] pages explain how ocx stores and resolves packages.
